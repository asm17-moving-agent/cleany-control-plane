Seat selection opens RIGHT panel with selected seat, robot selection, command selection, priority, request submission. The map and selected seat remain visible alongside both sidebars. No separate giant task-request tile.
Robot condition and human attention: show immediate issues separately from result review; problem robot also highlighted in list. Include compact entry points to pending/active request status and recent completed results. Summaries and actions, not fully expanded reports or photo comparisons.
